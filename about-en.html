About Us

Welcome to Bar 8 AM

We're proud to serve the finest cocktails, mocktails, wines, and beers in Ōmiya. Whether you're winding down after work or celebrating with friends, our cozy atmosphere and curated drink menu will make every visit special.

Find Us

  1. Head to Ōmiya Station East Exit
  2. Walk straight for 5 minutes
  3. Turn left at the Lawson convenience store
  4. We're on your right, next to Hotel Ōmiya

Meet the Owner

Akira has been passionate about mixology for over 15 years. After training in Tokyo's top cocktail bars, he opened Bar 8 AM to share his love of craft drinks with the Ōmiya community.

"Every drink tells a story. I want my guests to experience something new with every sip." — Akira
